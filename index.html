About

Between Japan
and Silicon Valley.

I’m a Specially Appointed Assistant Professor at the University of Fukui’s Regional Revitalization Headquarters. I teach entrepreneurship and AI and work with local governments across Hokuriku.

I’ve been Founder and CEO of Virufy since 2020.

My 15+ years in tech and AI include leading engineering teams in Silicon Valley and Tokyo at VMware Pivotal Greenplum and HP Enterprise, and serving as Technical Lead at MIT Sandbox.

Fluent in Japanese and Urdu/Hindi. One Young World Japan Ambassador and Eiheiji Town Evolution Ambassador.

Research

AI that works in the real world.

From hospitals to tourist towns to bank security centers, my work starts with real people and real data.

  • Medical AI

    Machine-learning models that screen respiratory health on an ordinary phone, validated in clinical studies in five countries.

    US Patent 12,444,502

  • Tourism and data analytics

    A sensor-fusion engine that forecasts visitor demand across Fukui’s tourist sites, built with local governments.

    Measurement (Elsevier) · Live dashboard

  • Behavioral nudges

    How nudges, not payment, move people to contribute data at scale. More than 256,600 study participants enrolled, unpaid.

    Journal of Behavioral Economics for Policy

  • Information security

    Security analytics at HP ArcSight for Fortune 500 clients, and security operations for Japanese megabanks.

    HP Enterprise

  • Bridging Japan and the world

    Connecting universities, governments and companies across Japan, Silicon Valley, the Gulf and South Asia.

    JST Sakura Science · LEAP 2025 · One Young World

  • Leading ventures

    What motivates volunteer teams, and how university ventures find their market.

    Future Business Journal · Strategic Change
